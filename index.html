Trinity Presbyterian Church Celebrating 50 Years of God’s Faithfulness

Our Mission

At Trinity Presbyterian Church, God is at work, in and through His church, to Gather His people, Grow them to maturity in Christ, and Go out to love God, neighbor, and one another—all to the Glory of God.

For 50 years, we have sought to be a faithful local congregation at a global crossroads. From the steps of UVA to the ends of the earth, Trinity has spent a half-century proclaiming Christ and serving His church. As we celebrate this Jubilee, we honor the generations who have walked before us and invite you to join the mission that continues today.
