IV.

Glorify

“To them God chose to make known how great among the Gentiles are the riches of the glory of this mystery, which is Christ in you, the hope of glory.”

Colossians 1:27

For 50 years, our worship has been a tapestry of tradition and innovation — from the hushed beauty of Lessons and Carols to the vibrant energy of original songs born from our own journeys of faith.

As we celebrate this Jubilee, we don’t just look back at what we have done; we look up at what God is doing. We worship a God who is at work, in and through His church, and we offer Him the next 50 years with joyful anticipation.
