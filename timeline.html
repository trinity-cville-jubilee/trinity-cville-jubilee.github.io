Our Timeline

Fifty years of God’s faithfulness, from our first worship service to today.

  1. 1976

    August 1 — First worship service held in the Baptist Student Center with Founding Pastor Skip Ryan.

  2. 1982

    First worship service is held in the new building, with 1,000 attendees.

  3. 1987

    Wednesdays in the Word Women’s Bible Study (now Women Together in the Word) begins.

  4. 1993

    John Hall installed as Senior Pastor.

  5. 1996

    Seeking to serve our neighbors, Trinity begins Abundant Life Ministry in Blue Ridge Commons.

  6. 2000

    RUF (Reformed University Fellowship) begins at UVA.

  7. 2003

    Trinity Fellows program begins with the arrival of its first class.

  8. 2003

    First International Thanksgiving Dinner hosted at Trinity for international students at UVA.

  9. 2006

    Greg Thompson installed as Senior Pastor.

  10. 2016

    Annual Vacation Bible School tradition continues with Marketplace VBS.

  11. 2017

    Trinity’s Friends for Life ministry hosts Night to Shine.

  12. 2017

    Walter Kim installed as Pastor for Leadership.

  13. 2022

    Women’s Council established.

  14. 2023

    Chris Colquitt installed as Senior Pastor.
